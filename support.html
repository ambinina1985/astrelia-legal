Frequently asked questions

Why isn't my Ascendant shown?

The Ascendant depends on your exact birth time. If you didn't enter it, Astrelia doesn't make it up. Add it in Profile › Details & birth data.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

In Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or from Profile › Subscription in the app. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period.

I changed iPhones: how do I get Premium back?

Open the Premium screen or Profile › Subscription and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple: request it at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Profile › Delete my data erases everything, on the device and in the iCloud backup. See also the Privacy Policy.

I don't get the morning reminder

Turn it on in Profile › Notifications and check that Astrelia notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings.
