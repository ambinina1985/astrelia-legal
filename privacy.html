3. Apple services used by the App

Birth city search

To find the coordinates and time zone of your place of birth, the text you type in the search field is sent to Apple Maps (MapKit). This processing is carried out by Apple under its own privacy policy. No other birth data is sent.

iCloud backup (if enabled)

If iCloud backup is available and iCloud is turned on on your device, your profile and saved people are copied to the private iCloud storage linked to your Apple ID, so you can find them on another iPhone. We have no access to this data.

In-app purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive neither your payment details nor your identity. The App verifies your subscription directly with the App Store, on your device.

Notifications

Reminders (daily horoscope, lunations, key dates, end of free trial) are scheduled locally on your iPhone, only if you allow them. No notification server is used.

Photos and sharing

When you save a card, the App asks for permission to add an image to your photo library; it reads nothing from it. When you share a card, you choose the destination app through the iOS share sheet. Cards never show your birth date, time or place; your first name appears only if you turn it on.

4. What we don't do

  • No account creation, no MyZodia server receiving your data.
  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no advertising identifier.
  • No third-party statistics or analytics tools.
  • No transfer of your data to any artificial intelligence service.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties.

5. Legal basis

Data is processed on your device to provide the service you request (performance of a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Optional features (notifications, iCloud backup, photo access) rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in iPhone Settings.

6. Retention

Data is kept on your device for as long as you use the App. It is deleted:

  • via Profile › Delete my data, which erases the data on the device and the iCloud copy;
  • by uninstalling the App (local data); any iCloud copy can also be deleted from Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

The text usage history only keeps the last 60 days.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your data. Since your data stays on your device, you exercise these rights directly in the App: edit in Profile › Details & birth data, erase via Profile › Delete my data. For any question, write to us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

You may also lodge a complaint with your country's data protection authority (in France: the CNIL).

8. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data about them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy, in particular if new features are added (for example an online account). The update date appears at the top of this page; we will inform you in the App of any significant change.
